# Vigil

A night watch played in the browser. At 03:12 you are the one person awake at Home on Earth, keeping watch over nine places run almost entirely by robots. Kirra's survey party on 16 Psyche drills into a new cut; you watch their cameras, read their short wireless reports, and when the reports stop, choose how far to send the last robot.

Live at https://ryanallen.com/vigil/. One hand-written page, no framework, no build. The photographs become live tilt-shift views through `app/depthview.js`.
